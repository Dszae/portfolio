'''Dipesh Sapkota''' ({{lang-ne|दिपेश सापकोटा}}; born in Kathmandu, Nepal) is a Nepali computer engineering student, artificial intelligence and machine learning enthusiast, and creative motion designer. He is an undergraduate at the [[Thapathali Campus]] of the [[Institute of Engineering]], [[Tribhuvan University]], and is known for developing educational tools and software applications including the ''IOE Admission Hub'', ''Git Visualizer'', and ''Sportivo''.

== Early life and education ==
Sapkota was raised in Kathmandu, Nepal. He completed his secondary education in science before enrolling in the Department of Computer and Electronics Engineering at [[Thapathali Campus]], Institute of Engineering (IOE), Tribhuvan University, pursuing a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Computer Engineering.

== Technical work and projects ==
During his undergraduate studies, Sapkota created several open web applications and engineering utilities:

* '''IOE Admission Hub''': An educational admission analytics portal and rank predictor designed to assist engineering entrance applicants across Nepal in evaluating historical cutoff ranks, seat availability, and campus priorities across Tribhuvan University constituent campuses.
* '''Git Visualizer''': An interactive educational application that models version control systems, commit graphs, and directed acyclic graph (DAG) operations visually to help students and developers understand Git internal states.
* '''Sportivo''': A real-time sports discovery and live-score web application featuring automated schedule synchronization, multi-server redundancy, and responsive match tracking interfaces.
* '''BeatMarker''': An automation utility designed for Adobe After Effects that performs audio frequency analysis to generate markers synchronized with audio rhythm and transients.

In addition to software and algorithm research, Sapkota has worked as a freelance motion graphics designer and video editor, producing promotional content and motion design assets for educational organizations.
